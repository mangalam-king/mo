2. Assign questions

Choose an exam and assign a unique set to one student, several students, or upload a batch. Each question needs text, four options, correctIndex (0–3), and optional explanation.

For bulk mode, paste a JSON array like: [{"studentId":"MO123","questions":[{"text":"2+2?","options":["2","3","4","5"],"correctIndex":2,"explanation":"2+2=4"}]}]

No Google Form is used. The built-in bank is saved directly to your MO Olympiad Firebase database when you enable the exam for students.
